# Support · Pixel Football

Pixel Football is a quarterback career game: you play every one of your downs,
the pass is a slingshot, the sack is physical, and off the field there are
contracts, the draft, the market and a house to pay for. Teams and players are
fictional.

## Something not working?

Write to us at **gonzoas96@gmail.com** with:

1. Your phone model and system version.
2. What you were doing when it happened (screen, play, week of the season).
3. A screenshot or a video, if you can.

We answer in days, not weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is my save stored?** Only on your phone. If you delete the app, the
save is deleted too. There is no cloud and no account.

**I bought a pack and didn't get it.** Close and reopen the game: pending
purchases are credited on launch. If it still doesn't show up, write to us with
your Apple or Google receipt and we'll sort it out.

**Can I get a refund?** Purchases are charged by Apple or Google, so refunds
are requested from the store (App Store → your account → Purchases; or Google
Play → Orders). We never see your payment method.

**Are there ads or tracking?** No. No ads, no analytics, no account. See the
[privacy policy](privacy).

**The game runs slowly on my phone.** In Settings → Camera, try the FAR angle,
and close other apps. The first game after installing takes longer because it
is baking both teams' uniforms; after that it goes straight in.

**Why aren't there NFL teams?** Because we don't have a license and we're not
going to pretend we do. The cities are real; the nicknames, logos and players
are ours.
