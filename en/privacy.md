# Privacy policy · Pixel Football

_Last updated: October 3, 2026._

Pixel Football is a quarterback career game for iPhone and Android made by
Gonzalo Álvarez. This page explains, briefly and with no fine print, what the
game does with your data. The long answer is: almost nothing.

## What the game does NOT do

- **It doesn't ask for an account or password.** No sign-up, no login, no email.
- **It doesn't collect personal data.** No name, email, location, contacts,
  photos, microphone or camera.
- **It has no ads** and no advertising trackers.
- **It doesn't use third-party analytics.** We don't know how much or how you play.
- **It doesn't sell or share data** with anyone, because it doesn't have any.

## What is stored on your phone

Your save (your quarterback, his career, the game settings) is stored **only on
your device**. If you delete the app, the save is deleted. There is no copy on
any server of ours.

## Game Center (iPhone only)

If you have Game Center turned on, the game reports **achievements** to Apple so
they show up on your Game Center profile. That profile belongs to Apple and is
governed by Apple's privacy policy; the game receives none of your Game Center
data back. You can turn it off in iOS Settings and the game works exactly the
same.

## In-app purchases

The game's store sells packs of points and in-game money. Payment is handled by
**Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play)** through your store account; we
never see or store your payment method. Purchases are consumable: they are
credited to your save instantly and do not transfer between devices.

## Children

The game collects no data from anyone, whatever their age. It contains no adult
content and is rated for all ages.

## Changes

If the game ever started collecting any data (for example, for an online
leaderboard), this page would change BEFORE that happened and would say so at
the top, with the date.
