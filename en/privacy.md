# Privacy policy · Pixel Football

_Last updated: November 26, 2026: your save now has a cloud backup._

Pixel Football is a quarterback career game for iPhone and Android made by
Gonzalo Álvarez. This page explains, briefly and with no fine print, what the
game does with your data.

## What the game does NOT do

- **It doesn't ask for an account or password.** No sign-up, no login, no email.
- **It doesn't collect your contact details.** No name, email, location, contacts,
  photos, microphone or camera.
- **It has no ads** and no advertising trackers.
- **It doesn't use third-party analytics.** We don't know how much or how you play.
- **It doesn't sell or share data** with anyone.

## What is stored on your phone

Your save (your quarterback, his career, what you bought in the game) is stored
on your device. Settings live only there.

## The cloud backup

So you don't lose your progress when you delete the app or change phones, the
game uploads a copy of your save to our server (Supabase, in the United States).
There is no sign-up: the copy is tied to a **random key** the game creates and
keeps on your phone (on iPhone, in the system keychain), not to your name or
email. The copy holds what is in your save, including the name you gave your
player. It is only used to give your progress back to you: it is not sold, not
shared, and not used for advertising or to track you.

You can **delete it whenever you want** in the career menu → Settings → Cloud
backup → Delete my backup. You can also ask by email with your code.

## Game Center (iPhone only)

If you have Game Center turned on, the game reports **achievements** to Apple so
they show up on your Game Center profile. That profile belongs to Apple and is
governed by Apple's privacy policy; the game receives none of your Game Center
data back. You can turn it off in iOS Settings and the game works exactly the
same.

## In-app purchases

The game's store sells packs of points and in-game money. Payment is handled by
**Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play)** through your store account; we
never see or store your payment method. What you buy is credited to your save
instantly and travels with it in the cloud backup.

## Children

The game asks no one for personal data, whatever their age. It contains no adult
content and is rated for all ages.

## Changes

If the game ever started collecting any other data (for example, for an online
leaderboard), this page would change BEFORE that happened and would say so at
the top, with the date.
